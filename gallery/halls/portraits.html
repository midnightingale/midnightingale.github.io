portraits

In the summer of 2025, I travelled to Paris for a portrait intensive with Studio Escalier. My friend Grace and I rented a small flat in Montmartre and sketched and painted for 5 hours a day, interspersed with critiques and live demonstrations. I learned so much about form and light and colour, and fell in love with portraiture.

one of the things I miss most about paris is the supermarchés on every other street. if I wanted some cheese I could just pop downstairs. there was a pâtisserie across the street from the studio and the cashier in there was so pretty and so nice to me, with my shy french and my indecision. marie went in with us once - she bought six chouquettes to share, no hesitation, and told us about her past lives as a sushi chef and a dilettante here and there. we were the same age, but we'd lived such different flavours of life
